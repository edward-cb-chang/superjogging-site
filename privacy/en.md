# SuperJogging Privacy Policy

[日本語](ja) · [繁體中文](zh-Hant) · [简体中文](zh-Hans)

Effective date: October 20, 2026

SuperJogging ("the app") is developed by Nex. Our principle is simple: **your running and health data stays on your device.** We have no servers of our own, and we never sell, rent or share your personal data.

## 1. What data the app handles

| Data | Source | Purpose | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|---|
| Run records (time, duration, beat, distance, route, notes, plan progress) | Your runs in the app | History, statistics, reports | Your device; with iCloud sync (Pro) on, also your own private iCloud database |
| Heart rate, heart-rate zone time, effort (1–10) | Apple Watch, Bluetooth heart-rate strap, the Health app | Shown during runs, heart-rate limit alert, reports | Only on your device and in the Health app — **never synced to iCloud** |
| Birth year, body mass, resting heart rate, active energy | The Health app (with your permission) | Heart-rate zone estimate, health report, calorie estimate | Only on your device; read when used, not stored separately |
| Steps and cadence | iPhone motion sensors (with your permission) | Actual cadence and step count | Only on your device |
| Location | GPS (outdoor mode only, with your permission) | Distance, pace and route | Only on your device |
| Settings (goals, beat, reminder times, etc.) | Set by you in the app | Make the app work the way you like | Your device; synced when iCloud sync is on |
| Purchase status | Apple App Store | Whether Pro is unlocked | Your device (Apple handles the transaction; we never see your payment details) |

The app **never** uses any health data (HealthKit data, heart rate, effort, body mass, etc.) for advertising or marketing, and never gives it to any third party.

## 2. Writing to the Health app

With your permission, the app saves each run to the Health app (the workout and its distance; on Apple Watch, also the effort you choose). This data is managed by Apple's Health app, where you can view or delete it at any time.

## 3. Ads (free version only)

The free version shows Google AdMob ads on the run summary and at the bottom of the history list (never during a run). After you buy Pro, no ads are loaded.
- After your first completed run, the app asks for ad consent (Google UMP, where required in your region), then iOS asks whether you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency).
- If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to show more relevant ads; otherwise only non-personalized ads are shown.
- Google's ad SDK may collect device information, approximate location (from IP address), ad interactions and diagnostics, as described by Google.
- Ad requests **never include** any running or health data.
- You can change your consent any time in Settings → About → Ad privacy options, or turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
- Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

## 4. Apple Intelligence (Pro, supported devices)

The AI coach (run feedback, weekly report, monthly summary, etc.) is generated by the **on-device** Apple Intelligence model. Your data never leaves your device; the app uses no cloud AI service.

## 5. iCloud sync (Pro, off by default)

When turned on, run records and settings are stored in your Apple ID's private iCloud database, which only you can access — the developer cannot read it. Synced records **exclude** heart rate, effort, heart-rate zone time and distance recorded by Apple Watch. You can turn it off any time in the app's settings.

## 6. Notifications

Reminders are notifications scheduled locally on your iPhone and never pass through a server. Notification permission is requested only when you turn reminders on.

## 7. Export and sharing

You can choose to export CSV / PDF files or share images. Exported files may include heart rate and effort, so check before sharing. Where you share is up to you; the app never uploads anything automatically.

## 8. Retention and deletion

- Data stays on your device until you delete the records or the app.
- Deleting the app deletes its data on your device.
- Data in iCloud can be deleted in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.
- Data the app wrote to the Health app can be deleted in the Health app.
- Because we have no servers and hold none of your data, we cannot look it up or delete it for you; the steps above delete it completely.

## 9. Your rights and choices

You can withdraw Health, Location, Motion & Fitness, Bluetooth, Notifications and Tracking permissions any time in iOS Settings; the related features stop and everything else keeps working. Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or Japan's APPI), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data. Since your data is on your device, you can do this directly in the app or as described in section 8, or contact us.

## 10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we update the effective date on this page; significant changes are announced in the app.
